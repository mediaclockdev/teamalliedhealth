Early Support, Lifelong Growth

Our early intervention team uses play-based, evidence-informed strategies to help young children build communication, motor, social and developmental skills — giving them the strongest possible start and supporting families every step of the way.

Book An Appointment Learn More →

What is early intervention?

Early intervention provides assessment and individualised support for young children who may need extra help with their development, communication, social skills, play, learning or everyday activities — whether due to developmental delay, disability or emerging concerns. The earlier we understand a child’s needs, the sooner we can put the right supports in place to help them thrive.

Every child deserves a strong start.We’re here to help them grow.

Areas we support

  • Developmental Milestones
  • Play & Interaction
  • Social Skills
  • Learning & Attention
  • Daily Living Skills
  • Family Coaching

Why early support matters

Early intervention can make a real difference by helping children:

  • ✓

    Build foundational skills

    Support key developmental, communication and learning skills from an early age.

  • ✓

    Support confidence and connection

    Help children feel more confident in themselves and their relationships with others.

  • ✓

    Strengthen everyday independence

    Develop practical skills for home, school and community settings.

  • ✓

    Help families feel equipped

    Provide guidance, strategies and ongoing support for parents and caregivers.

NDIS participants welcome

Team Allied Health is a registered NDIS provider. Early intervention can be funded through Capacity Building supports in your NDIS plan, whether you are plan-managed, self-managed or NDIA-managed. Contact us to talk through your funding.

Our approach

Practical, play-based support built around your child and family.

  1. 01

    Understand Your Child

    We learn about your child's strengths, needs and your family's goals.

  2. 02

    Set Meaningful Goals

    We work with you to create individualised, functional goals that matter to your family.

  3. 03

    Learn Through Play

    We use evidence-informed, play-based strategies to build skills in natural and engaging ways.

  4. 04

    Review Progress Together

    We regularly check in, celebrate progress and adjust supports as your child's needs evolve.
