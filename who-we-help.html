Life stage pathways

Individuals & Life Stages

From early childhood exploration to lifelong autonomy, our therapy meets you right where you are. Every stage has unique goals, strengths, and milestone markers.

0–6 Years

Early Childhood

Nurturing foundational developmental milestones, motor play, speech sounds, sensory regulation, and kindergarten readiness in a fun, exploratory environment.

Core focus areas:

  • Speech & Sounds
  • Gross & Fine Motor
  • Sensory Play
  • Social Play
Know more →
7–12 Years

School-Aged

Building classroom engagement, literacy confidence, emotional regulation, peer friendships, and physical coordination at school and home.

Core focus areas:

  • Handwriting & Focus
  • Social Navigation
  • Emotional Toolkits
  • Postural Control
Know more →
13–21 Years

Teens & Young Adults

Fostering practical life skills, executive functioning, self-advocacy, community navigation, and smooth transitions into secondary education or vocational pathways.

Core focus areas:

  • Daily Life Skills
  • Executive Function
  • Self-Advocacy
  • Vocational Prep
Know more →
21+ Years

Adults & Seniors

Rebuilding or preserving functional capacity, stroke and neurological rehabilitation, mobility maintenance, and adaptive equipment for active community participation.

Core focus areas:

  • Functional Capacity
  • Mobility & Falls
  • Assistive Tech
  • Neuro Rehab

Families & Support Networks

Meaningful progress does not happen in isolation. We actively support the parents, educators, and key caregivers who form the foundation of our clients' everyday environments.

Parents & Caregivers

Therapy should make home life calmer, not more complicated. We focus on practical, low-stress routines that weave skill-building into everyday family life— from breakfast chats to bath time transitions.

  • Transparent session summaries and actionable home practice guides.
  • Guidance on sensory calming spaces and positive behavioural support.
  • Empathetic, jargon-free consultations answering all parental questions.
Parent Resources & Guides →

Schools & Early Learning Centres (ELCs)

We collaborate directly with teachers, integration aides, and kindergarten directors across Melbourne to bridge clinical goals into real classroom success and playground social ease.

  • Onsite classroom observations and teacher strategy coaching.
  • Classroom sensory accommodation recommendations and seating aids.
  • School readiness screenings and transition support to prep.
