Participant Charter

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This charter sets out what you can expect from Team Allied Health, and what we ask of you, so that every participant, family and carer receives safe, respectful and effective care. It applies to everyone who receives our services, including the parents, carers and nominees who support them.

On this page

  1. 1. Your rights
  2. 2. Our commitments to you
  3. 3. Your responsibilities
  4. 4. Feedback and complaints
  5. 5. Contact us

1. Your rights

  • Respect and dignity — to be treated with courtesy and respect, free from discrimination, abuse, neglect and exploitation.
  • Choice and control — to be involved in decisions about your care, set your own goals and choose who supports you.
  • Information — to receive clear, jargon-free information about your assessment, therapy options, fees and progress.
  • Consent — to give or withdraw consent for any service at any time.
  • Privacy — to have your personal and health information kept confidential, as set out in our Privacy Policy.
  • Culturally safe care — to have your culture, language, beliefs, identity and neurodiversity respected.
  • Support — to have a family member, carer, advocate or interpreter with you.
  • Feedback — to give feedback or make a complaint without it affecting your care.

2. Our commitments to you

  • Deliver evidence-based therapy through qualified, AHPRA-registered clinicians (where registration applies) and supervised Allied Health Assistants.
  • Work with you to set clear goals and review your progress regularly.
  • Coordinate between our clinicians so you have one consistent plan.
  • Arrive on time for appointments and let you know as early as possible if we need to reschedule.
  • Explain fees before services begin and keep you informed about how your funding is used.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Treat our staff with courtesy and respect.
  • Give us accurate information about your health, funding and circumstances, and tell us about any changes.
  • Let us know at least 24 hours in advance if you need to cancel or reschedule (see our cancellation terms).
  • Provide a safe environment for home visits, including securing pets and letting us know about any hazards.
  • Take part in agreed home practice where you can, and tell us if something isn’t working for you.

4. Feedback and complaints

We welcome compliments, suggestions and complaints — they help us improve. You can contact us by email at info@teamalliedhealth.com.au or by phone on +61 412 345 678. We will acknowledge your feedback promptly and aim to resolve concerns quickly and fairly.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Health Complaints Commissioner (Victoria) on 1300 582 113 or at hcc.vic.gov.au.
