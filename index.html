Therapy Feels Like Fun Exploration

How We Turn Clinical Goals Into Engaging Play

Children learn, express, and develop best when they feel safe and engaged. Our clinicians combine proven clinical frameworks with playful games, tactile sensory toys, and everyday home activities so milestones feel like celebrations.

Sensory & Motor

Obstacle courses, putty, and balancing games for coordination & self-regulation.

Story & Speech

Interactive picture cards, puppets, and rhythm games that build clear speech & words.

Social & Emotion

Neurodiversity-affirming play to foster turn-taking, friendships, and calm transitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting therapy with Team Allied Health.

How fast can an assessment or appointment be scheduled?

We triage every referral within 24–48 business hours and will contact you to book the first available appointment with the right clinician.

Who can access your Allied Health Services?

Children, youth and adults funded through NDIS (plan-managed, self-managed or NDIA-managed), TAC, private health or private pay — across Greater Melbourne and via Telehealth Australia-wide.

Can multiple disciplines visit on the same day?

Yes. Our clinicians coordinate joint or back-to-back sessions (for example Speech and OT) so your family has fewer visits and one consistent plan.
