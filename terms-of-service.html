2. Referrals and intake

Referrals may be made by participants, parents, carers, Support Coordinators, GPs, Plan Managers and other professionals. Submitting a referral does not guarantee a service. We aim to triage referrals within 24–48 business hours and will contact you to discuss suitability, availability, funding and next steps.

If you refer someone else, you confirm that you have their (or their guardian’s) consent to share their information with us.

3. Our services

We provide allied health services including early intervention, speech pathology, occupational therapy, physiotherapy, psychology, behaviour support and Allied Health Assistant (AHA) support, delivered via mobile visits across Greater Melbourne and via Telehealth.

Services are delivered by appropriately qualified and, where required, AHPRA-registered clinicians. AHA sessions are delivered under the supervision of a qualified clinician. Before services start we will agree a service agreement or plan with you that sets out supports, pricing and how they will be delivered.

4. Fees, funding and payment

Our fees will be provided to you in writing before services begin.

  • TAC clients: services are billed to the TAC in line with its approved fees once your claim and services have been approved.
  • Private health: you pay at the time of service and may claim a rebate from your insurer, depending on your level of cover.
  • Private clients: payment is due on the terms stated on your invoice.

Travel for mobile visits may be charged in line with the applicable funding rules and your service agreement.

5. Cancellations and missed appointments

We understand that plans change. Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment, by phone or email.

Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice, and appointments missed without notice, may be charged at up to the full session fee. Where a session is funded by the TAC or another funder, cancellation charges are applied in line with that funder’s rules. Fees may be waived in genuine emergencies or illness.

If we need to cancel or reschedule, we will let you know as early as possible and offer an alternative time.

6. Telehealth sessions

For Telehealth sessions you are responsible for a suitable device, a stable internet connection and a private space. A parent or carer may need to be present for sessions with children. Clinicians may recommend face-to-face services if Telehealth is not clinically appropriate.

7. Safety and conduct

We are committed to safe, respectful, neurodiversity-affirming care. To keep everyone safe, we ask that home visit environments are free from hazards and that pets are secured. We may pause or end a session if a clinician’s or participant’s safety is at risk. We will work with you to find a solution before ending services.
