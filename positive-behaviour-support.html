Positive Behaviour for a More Independent Life

Our behaviour support team helps individuals and families understand behaviour, develop practical strategies, and create positive change.

Book An Appointment → Learn More

What is behaviour support?

Behaviour support helps individuals develop positive behaviour, build essential life skills and reduce behaviours of concern through evidence-based, person-centred strategies.

Respect. Understanding.
Meaningful change.

Our approach

We take a person-centred and collaborative approach, focusing on what matters most to the individual and their support network.

  • ✓Understand individual strengths and needs
  • ✓Develop practical, realistic strategies
  • ✓Work with families, carers and support teams
  • ✓Support across home, school, work and community
  • ✓Monitor progress and adjust as goals evolve

Who can benefit?

Behaviour support is for individuals of any age who need guidance to build skills, manage challenges and increase participation in everyday life.

  • Children

    Build early skills and positive routines.

  • Young People

    Develop independence and confidence.

  • Adults

    Achieve personal goals and greater participation.

  • Older Adults

    Maintain skills, routines and community involvement.

Our process

A clear and supportive journey, tailored to your needs.

  1. 01

    Get to Know You

    We listen to understand your goals and challenges.

  2. 02

    Develop a Plan

    We create a personalised behaviour support plan.

  3. 03

    Implement Strategies

    We work together across everyday environments.

  4. 04

    Review & Grow

    We track progress and adjust strategies as needed.

NDIS information

We are a registered NDIS provider. Behaviour support is funded under Capacity Building (Improved Relationships) and delivered by NDIS-registered practitioners. Plan-managed, self-managed and NDIA-managed participants are welcome. Ask us about funding.
