🎯 Therapy goals

What our therapies aim to achieve

Every plan starts with your goals. Across occupational therapy, speech pathology, psychology, physiotherapy and positive behaviour support, we focus on outcomes you can see in everyday life.

Build independence

Daily living, self-care and community skills that increase autonomy.

Communicate with confidence

Clearer speech, language and social communication at home, school and work.

Improve movement and wellbeing

Strength, mobility, emotional regulation and positive routines.

Measure real progress

Clear, measurable goals reviewed regularly and reported against your NDIS plan.

🗓️ How sessions work

What to expect from your sessions

A simple, supportive process from first contact to ongoing progress.

  1. 1

    Referral & intake

    Send a referral and we'll contact you to understand your needs and match you with a clinician.

  2. 2

    Initial assessment

    Your first session is a relaxed conversation and assessment to set goals together.

  3. 3

    Regular therapy sessions

    Practical, evidence-based sessions at home, in clinic, at school or online, with strategies to practise between visits.

  4. 4

    Review & reporting

    We review progress regularly, adjust your plan and provide reports for plan reviews.
