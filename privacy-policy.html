Privacy Policy

Team Allied Health Australia Pty Ltd (ABN [ABN]) (“Team Allied Health”, “we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information, including health information, in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), and the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic).

By using our website, submitting a referral or receiving our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide safe, effective allied health services. This may include:

  • Identity and contact details — name, date of birth, address, email, phone number, and emergency contacts.
  • Health information — medical history, diagnoses, assessment results, therapy goals, clinical notes, reports and correspondence from other health professionals.
  • Funding information — NDIS participant number and plan details, TAC claim number, Medicare and private health insurance details, and plan manager or support coordinator details.
  • Referrer details — when a GP, Support Coordinator, Plan Manager or other professional refers a participant to us.
  • Website information — information you submit through our online referral form, and basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited.

2. How we collect information

Wherever practical we collect information directly from you (or your parent, guardian or nominee). We may also collect it from third parties with your consent or where permitted by law, such as referrers, GPs and specialists, schools and early childhood services, the NDIA, TAC, plan managers and support coordinators.

You may choose to deal with us anonymously or under a pseudonym where it is lawful and practicable, but in most cases we need your identity to provide clinical care and process funding.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • assess referrals, triage enquiries and contact you about appointments;
  • plan and deliver therapy, including coordinated care between our clinicians and Allied Health Assistants;
  • prepare reports, including progress and plan review reports for NDIS, TAC or other funders;
  • process invoices and claims with funding bodies, insurers or plan managers;
  • meet our legal, professional (AHPRA and professional association) and NDIS Practice Standards obligations;
  • improve the quality and safety of our services, including handling feedback and complaints.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use health information for marketing.

4. When we disclose information

We only disclose personal information for the purpose it was collected, for a directly related purpose you would reasonably expect, with your consent, or where required or authorised by law. This may include disclosure to:

  • other members of your care team, such as your GP, specialists or school, with your consent;
  • funding bodies and their agents (for example the NDIA, NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, TAC, Medicare, private health insurers or plan managers);
  • service providers who support our operations (such as practice management, cloud storage and IT providers) under confidentiality obligations;
  • authorities where we are legally required to report (for example child safety concerns or a serious threat to life, health or safety).

Some of our software providers may store data outside Australia. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure the information is protected to a standard consistent with the APPs.

5. Storage and security

We store information in secure electronic practice management systems with access controls, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Telehealth sessions are conducted through secure video platforms.

Health records are retained for the periods required by law — in Victoria, generally at least 7 years after the last service, or for a child until they turn 25 — and are then securely destroyed or de-identified.

6. Accessing and correcting your information

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete. Please contact our Privacy Officer using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time (usually 30 days). In limited circumstances permitted by law we may refuse access, and we will explain why in writing.

7. Website, cookies and online forms

Information submitted through our online referral form is used only to process the referral. Please do not include more health information in the form than is needed for us to contact you; our intake team will collect further details securely.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to keep the site working and to understand general usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Our website may link to third-party sites, which have their own privacy practices.

8. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

9. Privacy complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact our Privacy Officer first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact:

  • the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner — oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992;
  • the Health Complaints Commissioner (Victoria) — hcc.vic.gov.au, 1300 582 113;
  • for NDIS participants, the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission — ndiscommission.gov.au, 1800 035 544.
